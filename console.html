What is real now

Production OpenEden TBILL metadata, Ethereum RPC health, OpenEden on-chain KYC, live issuer vault reads, mint/redeem availability, public CoW quote checks, routing and settlement preparation.

What remains gated

The final browser signing + broadcast step. 33Jane can prepare a real route and calldata, but it will not submit a transaction until the connected wallet passes issuer eligibility and the route is executable.
