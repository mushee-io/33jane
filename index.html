Execution stack

From RWA constraints to solver-ready, simulation-gated execution.

01 — Registry

RWA asset model

Canonical asset metadata, settlement modes, issuer data, quote currencies and provider-defined constraints.

02 — Eligibility

Constraint engine

Wallet claims, jurisdiction rules, trade limits and market-state checks before an order becomes executable.

03 — Liquidity

Universal adapters

Issuer mint/redeem, RFQ, AMM, provider bridge and CoW Orderbook liquidity through one interface.

04 — Routing

Execution graph

Direct and multi-hop routes that combine standard DeFi liquidity with permissioned RWA liquidity.

05 — Safety

Simulation certificates

Route continuity, quote freshness, execution-plan checks and optional eth_call validation before settlement.

06 — CoW

Solver candidates

Only routes with passing safety certificates are surfaced through the CoW sub-solver interface.
